Paneer Butter Masala

Paneer Butter Masala is a rich and creamy North Indian dish made with soft paneer cooked in a mildly spiced tomato and butter gravy. It pairs wonderfully with naan, roti, or rice.

Ingredients

  • 250 g paneer, cubed
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 3–4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • ½ tsp cumin
  • ½ cup cream
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 tsp kasuri methi
  • Fresh coriander for garnish

Steps

  1. Heat butter and oil in a pan. Add cumin, onion, and ginger-garlic paste. Sauté until the onion turns golden.
  2. Add tomatoes, turmeric, chili powder, and salt. Cook until the tomatoes become soft.
  3. Blend the mixture into a smooth paste and return it to the pan.
  4. Add a little water and simmer for 5–7 minutes.
  5. Add paneer, garam masala, cream, and kasuri methi. Cook for another 3–4 minutes.
  6. Garnish with fresh coriander and serve hot.
