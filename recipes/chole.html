Chole (Chickpea Curry)

Chole is a popular North Indian chickpea curry made with chickpeas simmered in a flavorful onion-tomato gravy and aromatic spices. It is delicious with bhatura, puri, roti, or rice.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked chickpeas
  • 1 large onion, finely chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • 1½ tsp coriander powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp chole masala
  • Salt to taste
  • 1–1½ cups water
  • Fresh coriander

Steps

  1. Heat oil in a pan and add cumin.
  2. Add onion and sauté until golden. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook for another minute.
  3. Add tomatoes, turmeric, chili powder, coriander powder, and salt. Cook until the tomatoes become soft and the oil starts separating.
  4. Add chole masala and garam masala.
  5. Add the cooked chickpeas and water. Mix well.
  6. Cover and simmer for 10–15 minutes, allowing the chickpeas to absorb the spices.
  7. Garnish with fresh coriander and serve hot.
